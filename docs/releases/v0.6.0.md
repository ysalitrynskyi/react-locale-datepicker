# 0.6.0

The bug-hunt release. An external review filed 86 findings against 0.5.1 in
one day; each was reproduced or checked against the code before it was
fixed, and all are resolved. The
ledger maps every finding to its commit and regression test:
[`docs/bug-hunts/2026-09-30.md`](https://github.com/ysalitrynskyi/react-locale-datepicker/blob/main/docs/bug-hunts/2026-09-30.md).

**This release changes behaviour you can see.** No prop is removed, but the
keyboard and focus model, some colours and some accessibility attributes are
different. Below 1.0 the minor number is the breaking slot, so `^0.5.x`
ranges do not pick this up on their own.

## Before you upgrade

Check these against your form; the full list is in the
[changelog](https://github.com/ysalitrynskyi/react-locale-datepicker/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#060--2026-10-01).

- **Enter submits the form** when the calendar is closed and nothing has
  been typed. It used to be swallowed.
- **`onBlur` fires once, when focus leaves the field and its calendar
  together.** It used to fire whenever the input lost focus, including when
  focus moved into the calendar. The typed draft still commits first and
  `onBlur` still receives the committed value.
- **Tab closes the calendar and moves on; ArrowDown enters it**, like a
  combobox (D21).
- **Escape is marked handled**, so a surrounding `<dialog>` closes the
  calendar only.
- **Tests that query by role or attribute:** `aria-expanded` moved from the
  textbox to the calendar button, the button's name now leads with the
  field's `aria-label` ("Start date, Open calendar"), the dialog is named by
  its month and year when the field has no `aria-label`, and the date echo
  is `aria-hidden`.
- **CSS:** the disabled calendar button is `[aria-disabled="true"]`, not
  `:disabled`; `text-transform: capitalize` is gone (the component
  upper-cases the first letter itself); several colours changed to meet
  WCAG AA in every theme; the compact field size applies only with a fine
  pointer.
- **Arabic and Persian locales show Latin digits** in the echo and month
  title, matching the day cells, unless the tag asks for a numbering system
  (`ar-u-nu-arab`, D20).

## New

- **`direction`** — `"ltr"`, `"rtl"` or `"auto"` (from the locale). Omitted,
  the picker inherits the page's direction as before (D19). A portaled
  calendar now matches its field's direction.
- **`id` and `aria-labelledby`**, forwarded to the input, to label the field
  without wrapping it in a `<label>`.
- Month and year views with one tab stop and a full arrow-key map.

## Fixed, in short

Dates in years 0–999 and from other realms; `UA` and `ua-UA` resolving to
Ukrainian; typed edits that could commit a date nobody typed; a draft that
outlived a new `value`, `form.reset()` or `disabled`; focus dropped on
`<body>` when switching views; a click guard that cancelled clicks anywhere
on the page; Safari 18.2+ reporting a finger as a mouse; portaled calendars
that fell behind their field, kept a stale theme or opened off-screen;
right-to-left placement; contrast, forced colours, print and 200% text.

## Verified

- Unit: 298 tests, green under `TZ=UTC`, `America/Los_Angeles`,
  `Asia/Tokyo` and `Asia/Kathmandu`; tests and e2e are now type-checked.
- Browser: Chromium at 320, 768 and 1280 px locally; Firefox and WebKit run
  in CI.
- Not verified on real hardware: the Safari 18.2 pointer workaround and the
  touch-tablet field size were checked by emulation, not on an iOS device,
  and no screen-reader pass was made.
