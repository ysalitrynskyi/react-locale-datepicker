# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticize code, not
people.

## Expected

- Treat people with respect regardless of experience, background, nationality,
  or the language they speak.
- Give and accept technical criticism gracefully. A rejected patch is not a
  rejected person.
- Remember that most contributors here are reporting problems in languages the
  maintainer does not read. That takes effort and is worth thanking people for.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through GitHub's private reporting on this repository, or by
opening an issue if the matter is not sensitive. Reports are handled
confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts for conduct
judged inappropriate. This is a small project without a moderation team; the
process is deliberately simple.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions — and when
representing the project elsewhere.

---

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
shortened to something a single maintainer can honestly enforce.
